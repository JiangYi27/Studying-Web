# 09-比较状语从句

> 比较状语从句表达"比…"、"和…一样…"、"越…越…"等比较含义，常由 than、as…as、the…the… 等引导。

---

## 一、形容词 / 副词的比较级

| 类型 | 构成 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 短词 | + er | smart → smarter；big → bigger |
| 长词 | more + 原级 | beautiful → more beautiful |

---

## 二、"比…"：than

- 口语化：He is smarter than me.　This carrot is bigger than that one.
- 更正式：从句补全主语（than + 主 + 谓语动词）：
  > **He is smarter than I am.**
  > **This carrot is bigger than that one is.**
  > **She is more beautiful than Snow White is.**

---

## 三、"同样…"：as + 原级 + as

> 正式：
> **He is as smart as I am.**（他和我一样聪明。）
> **This carrot is as big as that one is.**（这根胡萝卜和那根一样大。）

口语中常省略从句动词：
> He is as smart as me. / This carrot is as big as that one.

---

## 四、"越…越…"：the + 比较级 ...，the + 比较级 ...

> 结构：the + 比较级 + ..., the + 比较级 + ...

> **The more you practice English, the more fluent you become.**（你练习英语越多，就越流利。）
