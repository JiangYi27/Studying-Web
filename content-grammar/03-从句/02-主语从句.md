# 02-主语从句

> 主语从句 = 用一个**从句**作整个句子的主语。它位于谓语动词之前，通常以引导词开头。

---

## 一、基本结构

> **That the rabbit ate a carrot is obvious.**（兔子吃了根胡萝卜，这很明显。）

- `That the rabbit ate a carrot`：主语从句
- `is`：谓语
- `obvious`：表语

对比：`The fact is obvious.`（事实很明显。）——把主语 The fact 换成主语从句，就得到了上面的句子。

### 确定信息：由 that 引导

> **That the rabbit ate a carrot is a secret.**（兔子吃了胡萝卜这件事是个秘密。）

### 不确定信息：由 whether / who / what / where / when / how 引导

| 引导词 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| whether | Whether the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. |
| who | Who ate the carrot is obvious. |
| what | What the rabbit ate is obvious. |
| where | Where the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. |
| when | When the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. |
| how | How the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. |

> 引导词若本身是代词/副词，在问句中叫“疑问代词/疑问副词”；引导从句时，则叫“连接代词/连接副词”。从句除引导词外，其余部分的语序和中文基本一致（**陈述语序**）。

---

## 二、用 it 作形式主语

当主语从句过长，会造成“头重脚轻”，此时可用 **it** 作形式主语，把真正的主语从句放到句末：

> **It is obvious that the hungry rabbit had already eaten a carrot for dinner this evening.**（很明显，饿了的兔子今晚晚饭已经吃了根胡萝卜。）

下表中的 It 均指后面从句，本身没有实际意义，是**形式主语**；句末的从句才是真正的主语。

| 原句（主语从句在句首） | It 形式主语版 |
|---|---|
| That the rabbit ate a carrot is a secret. | It is a secret that the rabbit ate a carrot. |
| That the rabbit ate a carrot was discussed. | It was discussed that the rabbit ate a carrot. |
| That the rabbit ate a carrot happened. | It happened that the rabbit ate a carrot. |
| Whether the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. | It is obvious whether the rabbit ate the carrot. |
| Where the rabbit ate the carrot is obvious. | It is obvious where the rabbit ate the carrot. |
| Who ate the carrot is obvious. | It is obvious who ate the carrot. |

---

> 注意：主语从句引导词 `whether` 不可替换成 `if`——`if` 引导的主语从句**不能放在句首**，一般要用 whether；仅在作形式主语（it 开头）时偶尔可用 if。表示”一件事是否…”时，句首一律用 whether。
